## 2. What Circuit is, in one paragraph
Circuit helps people near each other help each other: students find nearby startups that hire for their skills, founders and
mentors find people who can help with what they need. We match on **what you need and what you can offer**, never on your
college name, gender, religion, caste or language. We never sell your data and we do not run third-party advertising trackers.

## 4. Who can see what
- **Other members** see your profile according to your privacy settings (public, members only, hidden). You choose whether your
  organisation can see you ("Visible to my organisation").
- **Moderators** (Circuit team, super admins) can read a direct message **only if someone reports it**. Every moderator action is logged.
- **Partner organisations** (colleges, incubators) see **aggregates**, counts under 5 are hidden, plus individuals only if they
  opted in or joined a cohort.
- **Investors and startups** see only what the matching and visibility rules allow. Promotions never change who is matched to you.
- **Promoted items** are shown by city, radius and capability only. We do not target by personal data or protected traits, and we
  keep no per-member ad tracking.
- Blocked people cannot see you in matches, the Daily Drop, Pulse, Live wire, intros, circuits or Inbox, and you cannot see them.

## 6. Children and under-18 members
If you say you are not yet 18, we apply safety defaults: your profile is hidden, you can post only to your own organisation,
you get no messages or requests from strangers, you are not shown in public Discover, you are not part of exchange circuits or
the Daily Drop, you see only promoted roles from verified startups, and we send you no email or push outside 09:00-19:00 IST.
You can see these in Settings → Privacy, and you cannot turn the protected ones off.

**[LAWYER]** Section 9 of the DPDP Act requires **verifiable consent of a parent or guardian** before processing a child's
personal data (and bans tracking and targeted advertising aimed at children). The product does **not** implement verifiable
parental consent yet. Decide: build it, or admit only 18+ in the first pilot. Until decided, the age screen only sets defaults.

## 8. Your rights and how to use them
Under the DPDP Act you can: get a summary and copy of your data, correct it, have it erased, withdraw consent, get your
grievance addressed, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or cannot.

- **Copy of my data:** Settings → Your data → Export. We email a link that works for 24 hours.
- **Correct my data:** edit your profile any time; or write to support.
- **Delete my account:** Settings → Your data → Delete. You are signed out at once; we then delete your sign-in, photos, profile,
  needs, offers and connections within `<DAYS>` days (target: minutes).
- **Withdraw consent:** delete your account, or write to us; some features need the data to work.
- **Complaint:** write to the Grievance Officer (section 1). If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Nominate someone:** write to support. **[LAWYER]** define the process.

## 9. Security

is on every table; staff use two-factor sign-in and every staff action is logged. If a personal-data breach happens we will
tell the Data Protection Board and affected members as the law requires. **[LAWYER]** confirm breach-notice wording.
