## What you are buying
A **promotion package**: for a fixed number of days, a profile, a role or a build log of your claimed and verified startup is shown in
clearly labelled **Promoted** spaces to people in the cities, radius and capabilities you choose. The price in ₹ is fixed per package and
shown before you pay. A promotion **does not** change matching, the Daily Drop, Live wire, circuits, search ranking or how applicants are ranked, and we
**do not guarantee** impressions beyond the cap shown, taps, applications, hires or any other result.

## How payment works
1. You choose a package in the app; we create an order for the price of that package (you cannot change the amount).
2. You pay on Razorpay Checkout (card, UPI, netbanking, wallet as Razorpay offers). Circuit never sees your card or UPI details.
3. Your promotion goes live **only after Razorpay confirms the payment**. Until then the app shows "processing". If you were charged
   and the promotion is not live within `<HOURS>` hours, write to support with the reference.
4. We email a receipt and a GST invoice (`<GST_RATE>`% `<included / extra>`). Invoice details: **[ACCOUNTANT]**.

## Cancelling after it started
**[BUSINESS DECISION]** Suggested: no refund for days already served; a pro-rata refund for unserved days only if we paused it or could not deliver it (for
example a fault on our side). A promotion can be paused or ended by us if it breaks the Terms or Community Guidelines; then we refund the unserved part.

## When we refund in full
- You were charged twice for the same order.
- The promotion could not run because of a fault on our side.
- Your promotion was rejected or removed by us for a reason that is not your fault. **[BUSINESS DECISION]**
