# Community Guidelines

**Version:** `<POLICY_VERSION>` · **Last updated:** `<DATE>`

Circuit works when people are real and generous. These are the few rules that keep it that way.

## Be real
- Use your own name and your real work. Build logs show what you actually made.
- Do not pretend to be someone else, a company, a college or a Circuit team member.
- Do not claim a startup that is not yours. If you do, we will unclaim it.

## Be generous, not noisy
- Ask for help clearly, thank the people who help, and answer intro requests, even if the answer is no.
- No spam: no copy-pasted messages to many people, no selling, no chain messages, no mass intro requests.
- No recruiting that hides the pay, the role or the company. Roles must be real and open.

## Be kind
- No harassment, bullying, threats, hate or slurs, or targeting someone for who they are.
- No sexual content. Nothing that sexualises or endangers anyone under 18.
- Keep other people's private details private. Do not post someone's messages, phone number or address.

## Keep it safe and legal
- No scams, fake offers, requests for money, or asking for fees to give someone a job or internship.
- No illegal content, no malware, no attempts to break, scrape or bypass Circuit.
- Under-18 members have extra protections. Do not try to contact them outside the rules the app sets. Adults who try will be removed.

## Reporting and blocking
Every post, profile and message has **Report** and **Block** within two taps. Blocking works both ways: you will not see each other anywhere.
Reports go to the Circuit moderators; the person you report is not told who reported them.

## What moderators do
After looking at a report we may **hide** or **remove** the content, **warn** the author, or **suspend** the account. Every moderator action is
recorded. We read a private message only if it was reported. If we got it wrong, write to `<SUPPORT_EMAIL>` and we will look again.

## Ranking is about skills
Matches, the Daily Drop and applicant lists are based on what people need and offer and on proof of work, never on college name, gender,
religion, caste or language. **Promoted** items are labelled and never change who is matched to you.
