## How to complain
1. In the app: Settings → Help → Write to us → choose **Grievance**. Or email the address above.
2. Tell us what happened and what you would like us to do. Do not send passwords or card details.
3. You get a reference (for example `S-1234`) by email straight away.

## What happens next
- We acknowledge your complaint within **`<24 hours>`**. **[LAWYER]**
- We aim to resolve it within **`<15 days>`**, and tell you what we did. **[LAWYER]**
- For a request to see, correct or delete your data you do not need a complaint: use Settings → Your data (export, edit your profile, delete your account).

## If you are not satisfied
