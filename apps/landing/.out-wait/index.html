Questions

Is Circuit free?+

Yes for students, founders, mentors and investors. Startups can pay to promote a role, always labelled as promoted.

Why is it invite-only?+

We are starting with one city and one partner college so every match is worth your time.

What do you do with my data?+

We use it to match you. We never sell it, and you can export or delete it any time from Settings.

I am under 18. Can I join?+

Yes, with a private profile by default and no messages from strangers.

Do you charge investors or founders a fee on deals?+

No. Circuit only makes introductions. No success fees, ever.
