Questions

How much time does it take?+

You set how many introductions you accept each week, and you can turn it off any time. A circuit asks for one specific favour, and you have 72 hours to say yes or no.

Can I say no?+

Yes. Every introduction and every circuit needs your consent.

Does Circuit cost anything for mentors?+

No. Mentors use Circuit for free.

Do I need to be verified?+

You can start with an email address. Verifying a work email adds a badge that helps founders trust a request from you.

What do you do with my data?+

We use it to match you. We never sell it, and you can export or delete it any time from Settings.
